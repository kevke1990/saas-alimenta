# Historical batch 2006–2012

## Scope

Build the oldest historical coverage from the official archive without assuming modern calculation rules.

## Required work

- Identify each published historical norm period.
- Capture need/capacity tables and period-specific appendix values.
- Identify transitions in KGB/child-regulation treatment.
- Identify tax, social-premium and minimum-income inputs actually used by the calculation adapter.
- Attach official provenance and effective dates to every executable value.

## Special rule

Where the available archive source is incomplete, retain `pending` and fail closed. Do not interpolate or backfill from a later year.

## Validation

Reference calculations and transition tests are mandatory before a period can become executable.

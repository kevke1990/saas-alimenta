# Release v0.9.7

Alimenta Pro v0.9.7 introduces the Professional Income Engine, intended to become a core product differentiator.

The engine supports structured gross/net/NBI input, annualisation, 2026 tax brackets and credits, pension deductions, IKB, holiday allowance, bonuses, overtime, benefits, KGB, optional IACK and auditable income components.

Source basis: Rechtspraak Rapport Alimentatienormen 2026 and official Belastingdienst 2026 rates/credits. The implementation remains an estimation aid and requires professional verification for production legal calculations.

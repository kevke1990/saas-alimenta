# Alimenta Pro v0.9.6

## Income Engine Professional

- Structured income profiles per parent.
- NBI, net-model and gross-model routes.
- 2026 Dutch Box 1 tax brackets and general/labour tax credits.
- Optional IACK, IKB/PKB, holiday allowance, 13th month, overtime, bonuses and taxable expenses.
- Pension, disability and other premium deductions.
- KGB and other net income as explicit components.
- Optional Box 3 taxable income input.
- Auditable annual bridge from gross income to estimated monthly NBI.
- Income warnings and calculation components exposed in the result.
- Engine version 0.9.6 stored in calculation output.

## Important

The income engine is a professional estimation aid. It does not replace an official Dutch tax return, payslip calculation or legal review. Individual facts and fiscal eligibility must be verified.

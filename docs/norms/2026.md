Key child-support formula above the non-AOW table threshold: `70% × [NBI − (0.30 × NBI + €1,365)]`.

The 2026 need table uses income points from €2,000 through €7,500.

Care discount: 5%, 15%, 25% or 35% depending on average care days, calculated over the table-based eigen aandeel of the parents in the children's costs and excluding extra costs. It is then deducted from the relevant parent's allocated share. In an insufficient-capacity scenario, the separate tekort/verzilverbaarheid rule applies.

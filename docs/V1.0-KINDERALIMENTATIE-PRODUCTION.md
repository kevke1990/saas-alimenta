# v1.0 Kinderalimentatie Production

## Production calculation chain
1. Determine relevant NBGI for the period; KGB must be included where the 2026 method requires it.
2. Determine the total own share from the 2026 needs table.
3. Add qualifying additional child costs.
4. Determine each parent's NBI and child-support capacity.
5. Perform capacity comparison only when joint capacity covers the total child cost.
6. Apply care discount to the base need, not additional special costs.
7. When joint capacity is insufficient, allocate available capacity without a normal capacity comparison and test whether the shortfall makes the care discount non-monetisable.
8. Persist the exact input snapshot, engine version, norm version and calculation fingerprint.

## Professional review required
The engine is deterministic, but legal classification remains a professional task. In particular verify historical NBGI, income components, earning capacity, special expenses, maintenance obligations, care arrangement and evidence.

## 2026 reference values
- Under AOW: formula from NBI €2,200: `70% × [NBI - (0.3 × NBI + €1,365)]`.
- AOW: formula from NBI €2,430: `70% × [NBI - (0.3 × NBI + €1,525)]`.
- Low income minimum: €25 for one child; €50 for two or more children.
- 2026 indexation of existing maintenance obligations: 4.6%.
